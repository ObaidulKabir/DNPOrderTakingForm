PrintPilot Administrator User Manual

Complete operational guide for feeding data, onboarding new print products, tuning pricing models, and managing courier logistics.

1. Making a New Product Operational

Saving a product under Products & Attributes is not enough. The storefront can detect it and ask questions, but automatic quotes stay held until you also feed supplier limits and the three pricing datasets for that Product ID. Work the steps in this order.

Operational means a customer can (1) type a keyword that selects this product, (2) finish the chat, and (3) receive an automatic quote for both urgent jobs (< 72h) and standard jobs (≥ 72h).

  1. Products & Attributes — Click + Create New Product. Set a unique slug (e.g. business-cards), names, VAT (0 or 0.20), distinctive NLP keywords, RFC hours, and every required attribute question (key, prompt, allowed values, quick-choice buttons). Click 💾 Save Product to PostgreSQL. Chat can start; it still cannot quote.
  2. Supplier Capabilities — Add at least one HRJ Media (hrj-media) row for the new Product. Enter quantity limits by hours, for example: 
    [{"maxHours":12,"maxQty":100,"choices":[25,50,100]},{"maxHours":24,"maxQty":250,"choices":[50,100,250]},{"maxHours":48,"maxQty":500,"choices":[50,100,250,500]},{"maxHours":120,"maxQty":1000,"choices":[100,250,500,1000]}]
    Click 💾 Save Supplier Capability. Optionally repeat for PrintedEasy.
  3. HRJ Historical Jobs (V3) — Feed 8–12+ completed vendor settlements tagged to this Product (do not leave Product on booklets). Use a spread of quantities, sizes, and hours-to-deadline. Click + Feed HRJ Job Record for each. This powers urgent cost.
  4. DNP Selling Obs (V1) — Feed matching historical customer selling prices for the same Product. Click + Feed Selling Price Record. This powers urgent customer price. Empty DNP data holds the quote.
  5. Route1 Standard Tariff — Upsert a supplier price for every Size / Quantity / Pages combination you want to auto-quote at ≥ 72h. The engine picks the smallest row whose size matches and whose quantity and pages are ≥ the job. Missing match → “No supported Route1 … cost”.
  6. Couriers & Postcodes — Shared across products. Only add a postcode if it is not already listed; otherwise the bot asks for mileage.
  7. Verify on the storefront — Open ← Open Live Quote Storefront. Prompt with a keyword (e.g. 250 A5 business cards … tomorrow by 4pm). Confirm the bot uses the new singular name, an urgent total appears, then repeat with a deadline four+ days out for the standard tariff path.

Size and Pages in the pricing feeds must match how the engine looks the product up:

  • Booklets: real A4/A5 and real page count.
  • Flyers: A4/A5 (A6 counts as A5); pages 4 single-sided / 8 double-sided.
  • Roller banners: Size always A4; pages 16 standard / 24 premium.
  • Any other new product: Size string in HRJ / DNP / Route1 must exactly equal the customer’s size choice, and Pages should be a complexity equivalent (default 16 if the product has no page count).

Full checklist, a business-cards worked example, and hold-reason FAQ: see ADMIN_USER_MANUAL.md in the project root (section 4).

2. System Architecture Overview

PrintPilot separates the Customer Conversational Storefront (/) from the Backend Decision & Pricing Engine (/api/*) and PostgreSQL (Relational + JSONB) Database. Every change you save in this Admin Dashboard immediately updates PostgreSQL and takes effect on the next customer quote or chat turn without restarting the server.

3. Onboarding or Editing a Print Product (Multi-Product Engine)

Navigate to Products & Attributes in the left sidebar to manage the product catalog:

  • Product ID & Names: Choose a unique URL-safe slug (e.g. perfect-bound-books or business-cards), plural display name, and singular name.
  • VAT Rate: Enter 0 for zero-rated print items (such as UK booklets and leaflets) or 0.20 for standard 20% VAT items (such as roller banners, posters, and stationery).
  • NLP Detection Keywords: Enter comma-separated words (e.g. flyer, flyers, leaflet). When a customer types a natural-language request on the home page, PrintPilot scans these keywords to automatically switch to the matching product schema.
  • Dynamic Product Attributes (JSONB): Click + Add Attribute Question to define the exact specification fields required for that product:
    • Attribute Key: Internal key stored in PostgreSQL JSONB (e.g. size, sides, lamination, pages).
    • Conversational Question Prompt: The exact question the AI bot asks in chat when this field is missing from the customer's initial prompt.
    • Input Type & Allowed Values: Choose select (dropdown), number, or text. For dropdowns, provide comma-separated allowed values (e.g. Single-sided, Double-sided). For numbers, you can enforce min, max, and multipleOf (such as multiples of 4 for saddle-stitched pages).
    • Quick Choices: Buttons displayed below the bot's question in chat so the customer can answer with a single click.

4. Managing Supplier Capabilities & Machine Exclusions

Navigate to Supplier Capabilities to control how suppliers (such as HRJ Media and PrintedEasy) handle each product:

  • Quantity Limits by Hours: Defines the maximum quantity that can be automatically quoted without human intervention based on hours remaining until deadline (e.g. up to 50 booklets within 12h, 100 within 24h).
  • Excluded Attribute Combinations: JSONB rules that automatically place an order into AMBER / Manual Check when a supplier machine cannot run a specific combination. Example for HRJ Media A4 Landscape booklets under 72h: 
    [{"attributes": {"size": "A4", "orientation": "Landscape"}, "maxHours": 72, "reason": "HRJ excluded: A4 Landscape cannot be produced on its machine."}]

5. Feeding Historical Pricing Data (HRJ V3 & DNP Selling V1)

Urgent jobs (<72 hours) are priced using two independent empirical models backed by PostgreSQL:

A. HRJ Historical Jobs (Vendor Settlement Model V3)

  • Go to HRJ Historical Jobs (V3) to add completed vendor settlement records (vendor_due).
  • The engine computes kernel similarity across quantity, page/complexity, size, and urgency bucket, blended with recency-weighted ridge regression.
  • It outputs an Expected Settlement and a Protected HRJ Allowance (max(expected + £35, P80)) used as the cost basis.

B. DNP Selling Observations (Customer Price Model V1)

  • Go to DNP Selling Obs (V1) to feed historical customer selling prices (sell).
  • The engine calculates weighted percentiles (P25, P50, P65, P75, P90) and selects P75 for ≤12h, P65 for ≤24h, or P50 for >24h, protected by a 2× fulfilment cost floor and the Deadline Compression Barrier (<18h).

6. Managing Standard Tariffs (≥72h Route1 Matrix)

When a customer's deadline is 72 hours or more away, PrintPilot switches to the Standard Price Matrix (Route1 Standard Tariff tab). You can filter existing rows or upsert new (Product, Size, Quantity, Pages, Supplier Price) combinations.

7. Managing Couriers, Distance Bands & Verified Postcodes

Go to Couriers & Postcodes:

  • Verified Postcode Distances: Add UK postcodes and their road distance in miles from HRJ Media (E3 4JN). Once a postcode is registered here, customers delivering to that postcode do not need a staging mileage estimate.
  • Courier Distance Bands: Edit Gophr and DNP baseline costs and DNP's mileage-sensitive post-RFC operational buffer hours for each mileage tier (0–3 mi through 35–80 mi).
